# ADR-005: Unions and Enumerated Types

## Status

💡 **Proposed**

> A proposed idea

## Context

Languages often support types 

Swift e.g. supports “enums with associated values,” conflating two concepts in one keyword. This might be good from their point of view, but not necessarily good for Clawr.

## Decision

Clawr will support two keywords: `enum` for simple enumerated values (maybe with a `string` representation and maybe with a default JSON representation) and `union` for sum-types (maybe with a tag to identify the current type.

A `union` type is a selection of different `data`structures rather than tuples.

## Rationale

1. To conceptually separate simple enumerations from sum types.

## Alternatives Considered

- `enum` with associated values à la Swift.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Clawr can spread quickly to many platforms.
- Compiler developers can perform different optimizations for different chipsets.
- New optimization ideas can be tested easily.
- Compiler developers can focus on either the frontend or the backend

### Negative

- Requires tight specification of, and adherence to, the CIR. Developers must be able to trust the ecosystem.
