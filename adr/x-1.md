# ADR-X01: `subset`

[ADRs](./README.md)

## Status

💡 **Proposed**

> A proposed idea

## Context

The `integer` and `real` types support lattices (subsets) based on ranges. Such a range might be useful to declare officially. The `truthvalue` type has only three values/states and supports enumerated lattices selecting any one, two or all of said states.

> [!warning]
> The `real` type is not yet formalised. It needs precision. Is that part of the lattice or a separate property? That might affect how we define `subset` here.

## Decision

The `subset` keyword defines a sublattice to the `truthvalue`, `integer` or `real` top lattices.

- `subset boolean = thruthvalue(false|true)`
- `subset natural = integer(1...)`

Subset declarations do not propagate to the CIR. They are only aliases used by the frontend.

## Rationale

- Easy reuse of lattices in declarations.
- Seems like an obvious feature as subsets/sublattices already play an important role.
